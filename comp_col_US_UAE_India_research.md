## 2) DUBAI, UAE — Tier 1/2 tech role

### Base salary (Levels.fyi, all-companies UAE SWE)
- Median TC **$94,758/yr (~AED 348K)**; 25th $65.4K; 75th **$124K (~AED 456K)**; 90th **$157K (~AED 577K)**.
- Senior at a Tier1/2 firm ≈ 75th–90th pct → **AED 25,000–40,000/month base (AED 300K–480K/yr; ~$82K–131K)**. Equity is rare/small in-region; comp is mostly base + housing/allowances.
- Source: https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/united-arab-emirates

### Personal income tax
- **0% — no personal income tax in the UAE** (confirmed; corporate tax 9% exists but not on salaries).
- Source: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/united-arab-emirates/individual/taxes-on-personal-income

### 1BR rent — Tecom / Dubai Internet City / Barsha Heights (2024–2025)
- Numbeo Dubai: 1BR city-centre **AED 9,175/mo** (range 7,000–15,000); outside-centre AED 5,584/mo.
- Tecom/Barsha Heights is a mid-central tech district → typical 1BR **AED 80,000–95,000/yr = ~AED 6,700–7,900/mo (~$1,815–2,150/mo)**. (Numbeo city-centre AED 9,175 skews high via Marina/Downtown.)
- Source: https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Dubai

### End-of-service gratuity (Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, Art. 51)
- Payable after **1 year** continuous service, on **last basic salary** (excl. allowances):
  - **21 days' basic pay per year** for the first 5 years;
  - **30 days' basic pay per year** for each year beyond 5;
  - total capped at **2 years' pay**.
- Source: UAE Government portal (u.ae — end-of-service benefits) + the Labour Law.

### Utilities (DEWA) + cooling — 1BR
- Numbeo basic utilities (85m², incl. electricity/water/cooling/garbage): **AED 906/mo** (range 540–1,500); internet AED 347/mo.
- For a 1BR (~55–75m²): DEWA electricity+water ~AED 400–700/mo; **district cooling (Empower/Emicool) AED 300–600/mo** where applicable → combined **AED 600–1,000/mo**.
- Source: https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Dubai

### Mandatory health insurance
- **Employer-paid and legally mandatory** (Dubai Health Insurance Law 11/2013; federal mandate extended to all Emirates from Jan 2025). Employee pays nothing for own basic cover.
- Employer cost: Dubai minimum "Essential Benefits Plan" ~AED 550–800/yr; typical plans AED 1,500–5,000+/yr.

### Effective income tax on ₹95L (new regime, incl. surcharge + cess)
- Surcharge slab ₹50L–1Cr = **10%** (confirmed); cess 4%. New-regime surcharge capped at 25%.
- **FY2024-25 (AY25-26):** tax ≈ ₹25.40L + 10% surcharge + 4% cess = **~₹29.06L → ~30.6% effective**.
- **FY2025-26 (AY26-27, new slabs):** ≈ ₹24.30L + surcharge + cess = **~₹27.80L → ~29.3% effective**.
- (₹75K standard deduction on salary trims taxable to ~₹94.25L, reducing tax ~₹20–22K.)
- Source (slabs + surcharge): https://cleartax.in/s/income-tax-slabs

### Rent — good 2BHK, ORR / Whitefield (2024–2025)
- Numbeo Bengaluru: 1BR city-centre ₹30,111/mo; 3BR city-centre ₹78,846/mo; 3BR outside ₹47,346/mo.
- A good/gated **2BHK in ORR (Marathahalli–Sarjapur) / Whitefield ≈ ₹35,000–60,000/mo** (newer gated communities ₹45K–70K).
- Source: https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Bangalore

### EPF employer contribution
- Employee 12% of basic + DA; **employer 12%**, split **8.33% → EPS (pension, capped on ₹15,000 wage = ₹1,250/mo)** and **3.67% → EPF**. Employer also pays 0.5% EDLI + admin charges.
- Statutory wage ceiling ₹15,000/mo, though many employers contribute 12% on full basic. Source: EPFO (epfindia.gov.in).

## Cross-market anomaly flags
- **US Levels medians overstate Bentonville:** $254K/$313K are Bay-Area-weighted; HQ Senior ~$190–210K, Staff ~$260–290K.
- **Dubai 0% income tax** but **no equity + employer-paid housing/health/gratuity** make base-only comparisons misleading vs US/India total-comp.
- **India Staff/P4 ₹92L** is high but consistent with Walmart Global Tech being a premium payer; ~₹95L sits at the P4/Principal boundary.
- **India effective tax ~30%** on ₹95L is close to the US household's ~24% AND higher than Dubai's 0% — the tax gap is the dominant driver of net differences.
